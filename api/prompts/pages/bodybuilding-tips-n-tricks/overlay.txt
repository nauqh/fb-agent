Image text panel (composited in post-processing — not drawn by the image model):

The hook is what goes on the panel. It is defined above; nothing is restated
here, because this file used to carry a second copy of its rules under a second
field name and the two drifted.

highlightPhrases (coloured highlights on the text panel):
* Return 1-3 exact substrings copied verbatim from the hook. Never more than 3.
* Use phrases of 2-8 words: the name of the exercise or movement, a rep or set count, a muscle group, a named lifter, the promised result — e.g. "three beginner exercises", "massive arms", "rapid arm growth", "without a single cable machine" as separate entries.
* Pick the phrases that carry the promise of the post. Fewer and longer beats more and shorter here.
* Keep each phrase short enough to sit on one line. The panel wraps at roughly 45 characters, and a phrase broken across two lines renders no gold at all.
